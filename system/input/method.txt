The method uses whole-line column generation to coordinate selective human-robot disassembly under shared resource capacities. Each column is a complete line plan satisfying task, precedence, cycle-time, fatigue, configuration, and recovery constraints. A two-phase restricted master coordinates human and robot demand, while persistent CPLEX line models generate improving columns. Complete exact pricing provides repaired dual upper bounds. Restricted integer solutions are checked against the original constraints before updating the lower bound. Demand-driven resource-profile MILPs and lower/upper dynamic programs address remaining integer gaps. The procedure reports verified solutions, valid bounds, proof states, and actual termination reasons within the specified time budget.
